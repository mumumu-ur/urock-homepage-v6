Spacecraft frame
↓
Digital forensics analysis equipment
↓
Holographic analysis interface
↓
Observation window
↓
Outer space
↓
Data Universe

The motion should feel cinematic, as though the camera is moving through the interior of the spacecraft and traveling toward the Data Universe beyond the observation window.

Apply a subtle 3D parallax effect that creates depth even with small mouse movements.

The Foreground layer should move more noticeably.

The interaction should remain refined and professional.
